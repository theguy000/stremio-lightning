goat

atrophy ›

/ɡoʊt/ · noun

die Ziege
Index:
01 Definition

In German, die Ziege is the animal. In modern sports and internet slang, G.O.A.T. is used universally across Germany for legendary performers (like Messi or Jordan).

02 In use
  • i
    The mountain goat climbed the steep rocky cliff easily.
    Die Bergziege kletterte mühelos die steile Felswand hinauf.
  • ii
    Many football fans consider Messi to be the G.O.A.T.
    Viele Fußballfans halten Messi für den G.O.A.T.
